# Changelog

## v1.2
- Faster repeat use: faculty lists are remembered per subject in your browser, so subjects you have opened before show their faculty instantly (they refresh automatically once VTOP loads the table).
- Your last-used semester is pre-selected (newest semester on first use), saving a click.
- Added a small loading bar while VTOP fetches the materials table.

## v1.1
- Faculty list now includes every professor across **all pages** of the materials table, not just the visible page.
- Faculty filter now filters the whole table across all pages.
- Fixed duplicate "Show entries" and "Search" boxes appearing above the table.
- Fixed the dropdowns disappearing when VTOP reloaded the page area after choosing a subject. Your selections are now kept.
- Faster loading: the faculty list appears as soon as VTOP finishes loading the table.
- Subject search now matches several words in any order.
- Lower CPU use while browsing VTOP.

## v1.0
- First version: semester, subject, and faculty dropdowns for Course Page Consolidated.
